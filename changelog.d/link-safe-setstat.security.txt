Add explicit link-safe attribute operations that modify a symbolic link itself and never its target: Session#lsetstat/#lsetstat! use the server-advertised lsetstat@openssh.com extension, the native version 4/5 LSETSTAT packet, or the no-follow version 6 SETSTAT, and raise Net::SFTP::UnsupportedOperation before sending anything when none is available rather than falling back to an ordinary link-following setstat. Session#open gains a no_follow option (version 6 NOFOLLOW flag) and the remembered handles it returns are the only handles accepted by the new Session#flsetstat/#flsetstat!, which otherwise raises Net::SFTP::UnknownHandle. Server-reported failures and unsupported attributes are preserved in the FXP_STATUS response.
