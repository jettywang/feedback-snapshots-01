BREAKING: Security: Session#open now raises ArgumentError before sending an FXP_OPEN packet for flags that cannot be represented safely, instead of silently widening or dropping them. IO::EXCL without IO::CREAT previously lost its exclusive-create guarantee (on v5/v6 the EXCL bit was discarded entirely), and IO::TRUNC without IO::WRONLY/IO::RDWR previously emitted a destructive truncate on a handle opened without write access. Both are now refused identically across protocol versions 1-6, so no packet reaches the server.
