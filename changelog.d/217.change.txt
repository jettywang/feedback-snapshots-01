Added Net::SFTP::Session#extended and #extended! for FXP_EXTENDED requests. An extension can only be invoked after the handshake completes and only if the server advertised it in that handshake's FXP_VERSION; otherwise Net::SFTP::ExtensionNotSupportedError is raised before any packet is sent, never silently rewritten as a weaker plain request. An advertised extension still succeeds or fails on its own response.
