Security: Attribute and directory-listing responses are now parsed against the structure their own flags and counts describe. Unknown attribute flags, fields shorter than their type, string lengths that exceed the packet, counts that exceed an enclosing ACL or extended-attribute structure, misplaced fields and trailing bytes raise `Net::SFTP::Exception` (or a subclass) instead of yielding an attributes object with nil or borrowed values; a rejected response fails its request without invoking the callback. In an `FXP_NAME` response, one entry's attributes can no longer consume bytes that belong to the next entry.
