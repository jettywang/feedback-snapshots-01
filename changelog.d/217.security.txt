Security: Malformed outer SFTP packet lengths are now rejected by the framing layer. A frame with a zero length, or one longer than 256 KiB (`Net::SFTP::Session::MAX_PACKET_LENGTH`), raises `Net::SFTP::Exception` as soon as its four-byte header arrives, before its body is buffered or a packet is allocated. Replies completed earlier in the same read are still delivered; requests still pending are failed with the framing error and the buffered tail is discarded instead of being reparsed as a new packet. Fragmentation is unchanged: splitting a valid packet across any number of channel-data callbacks, or delivering several packets together, produces the same responses and `pending_requests`.
