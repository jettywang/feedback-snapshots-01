`Session#open` now resolves its flags into one version-independent intent before encoding, so the same call no longer gains write access, truncates an existing file, or loses an exclusive-create requirement on a different SFTP version. `O_EXCL` without `O_CREAT` and `O_NOFOLLOW` on versions before 6 are refused before a packet is sent instead of being mapped to a wider permission set.
