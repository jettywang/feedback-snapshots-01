Fixed the pure-Python WebSocket reader rejecting a permessage-deflate
message whose compressed wire size is larger than ``max_msg_size`` while
its decompressed payload is not. The limit is now applied to the
assembled message and its decompressed size, so many small fragments
can no longer reset it. A fragmented message or frame cut off by EOF is
rejected instead of being published as a shorter message, and bytes
arriving after EOF can no longer complete it -- by :user:`aio-libs`.
