Fixed server-side WebSocket ``receive()`` cancellation during a concurrent
``close()`` discarding the internal closing marker, which made the handshake
fall back to an abnormal close (``1006``) instead of a clean one. The closing
state is now recorded before yielding to the parked receiver, and messages
already parsed but not yet delivered are retained for the next consumer rather
than lost or consumed by the wrong task -- by :user:`bdraco`.
